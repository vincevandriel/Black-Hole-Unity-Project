# Guided exploration and inspection HUD

The observatory starts with guided stops enabled. It pauses at five teaching bookmarks within the available replay: intact binary, inspiral and tidal gas, final approach, coalescence, and remnant. These are presentation bookmarks, not detected gas transitions. The first two stops retain two separate black holes. The source timeline, gas, bodies and wrist graphs all stop together; camera motion and the suit HUD keep running.

At a stop, right-drag to look around and use WASD/QE or the mouse wheel to approach. Tags identify the system, each black hole, the conditional gas and outgoing waves. A name in the left guide turns the view toward that target without moving the observer. Looking toward a target opens its square. Within 18 displayed gravitational radii, press F or click the square to read its explanation. Optional voice starts only through PLAY VOICE. Looking away, leaving the inspection range, continuing, seeking, switching scenario or leaving the exhibit closes the card and stops narration.

CONTINUE or Space resumes until the next stop. GUIDED STOPS switches between the tour and uninterrupted playback. RESTART rearms the stops. Manual seeks and reverse playback rearm future stops from the selected time. Narrow or late-start histories omit unavailable phases. A large frame step cannot skip the first unvisited bookmark.

The procedural square is imported from the original asset in the Codex chat **Create Unity HUD animation asset**, thread `01a16c9c-8fe4-7313-ae2f-edc44b8a6928`, created 24 October 2026. Only its runtime geometry/controller and adapted URP shader are included. It contains no Motion Array footage. The source preview, editor/demo scenes and validation files are excluded. The opening runs in reverse when gaze leaves; idle geometry first settles continuously to the opening endpoint. Interrupted closing resumes from its current geometry.

The original mesh is copied into the suit UI using reusable buffers so astronomical lensing does not distort the annotation itself. The observer camera supplies desktop gaze; this is not eye tracking or headset validation. Tags locate educational subjects and do not define extra physical objects. The system and wave anchors are annotation locations. The text and recordings distinguish the representative numerical replay from adjustable educational experiments, assumed gas from measurements, and magnified optical effects from literal wave visibility.

Narration uses bundled offline machine recordings. See [GUIDED_TOUR_NARRATION.md](GUIDED_TOUR_NARRATION.md) for the editable scripts, clip paths and replacement instructions. No speech service or synthesis process runs in the player. The player loads one requested narration clip at a time and unloads it on close.

Validation lives outside the desktop package. Pure tour tests cover timing, seeks, reverse playback and scenario generations; the opt-in development harness checks actual HUD geometry, gaze/proximity, cards, audio, five stops, shared clocks and cleanup. Release compilation excludes both guided QA helpers.

For the prepared reference, the five stops are −1000, −550, −80, 0 and +180 gravitational times relative to common-horizon formation. The first two retain separate bodies at separations of approximately 11.19 and 9.84 gravitational radii. Adjustable histories derive their bookmarks from their own duration and gas coverage.

On 24 October 2026, the portable tour suite passed 107 assertions. The development player passed 52 guided integration checks and the existing gas/rendering regression passed 77 checks with 240 rendered sequence frames. Reviewed images cover the intact binary, the dedicated inspiral view, final approach, coalescence, remnant and an open narrated inspection card. These checks exercise the actual procedural geometry, production rendering and playback state. They do not establish native mouse/headset usability, listening quality on every audio device, or sustained laptop performance. The Mac player requires separate native validation.
