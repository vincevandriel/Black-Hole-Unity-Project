# Public route to generated relativistic binary fluid fields

Public equations, source code and GW150914 measurements are sufficient to generate the missing simulation fields ourselves. No private gas dataset or user-supplied scientific export is required. The useful next step is a sequence of small, verified solvers, retaining the current interactive viewer while the physical model improves.

This route distinguishes the new generated two-dimensional fluid mode, a later relativistic fluid solver on an approximate binary metric, and a numerical solution of Einstein's equations. It does not count the new reduced mode as completion of M5 compatible scientific gas or M6 binary observer optics. Source and repository details were checked on **24 October 2026**; the build sequence and numerical gates below are proposed work, not results of a GRHD/GRMHD run.

## The generated fluid tier

The model specified in `GENERATED_FLUID_MODEL.md` advances height-integrated isothermal Euler equations using finite-volume Rusanov fluxes. Its sound speed, initial circumbinary ring, Plummer softening, sinks and outer boundary are declared assumptions. It derives surface density and planar momentum by evolving a PDE; it does not import a gas movie or prescribe every future density frame. Its cached states make direct seeking reproducible.

Its gravity uses same-epoch `InteractiveBinaryScenario` positions and mass fractions as an external Newtonian forcing surrogate. Using SXS coordinate tracks in that force law does not turn them into a relativistic metric. The Gaussian vertical display profile is an extrusion of surface density, not a solved third dimension. Fixed sound speed is a closure, not a solved temperature/energy history; density-linked brightness is not a calibrated luminosity. External forcing, sinks, outflow and numerical floor additions require explicit budgets. There is no gas backreaction, magnetic evolution or relativistic radiation transfer.

This is a substantive hydrodynamics step above the earlier prescribed ring/stream field. Near-horizon realism still requires a different gravity and fluid model; reducing a softening length or increasing a particle count cannot supply that model. Keep the physical grid independent of the rendering preset so two visitors selecting different image quality see the same computed experiment.

## What we can choose and generate

Select one consistent binary realization and record its mass ratio, spins, mass normalization, orbital convention, phase, time origin and eccentricity assumptions. A coherent posterior row and the fixed SXS representative reconstruction are different realizations. Source masses and source time must not receive the cosmological redshift factor twice.

Then choose the hypothetical environment: density normalization, pressure/internal energy or equation of state, angular momentum distribution, magnetic vector potential, boundaries, atmosphere, cooling and emission prescription. These are simulation inputs. GW150914 does not determine a unique gas environment.

The output fields follow from those inputs and equations. A simulation need not reproduce a previously stored solution to be a physical simulation, but it must pass numerical benchmarks and expose its approximation. Reproducing a published setup is a useful first independent check.

[Khan et al. 2018](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.02624v2), Sections III and V.A, provides a relevant model family: nonspinning holes with mass ratio 29/36 and several magnetized disks, evolved in full GRMHD. Case B has initial maximum H/R=0.22, inner disk radius 20 M and outer radius 100 M. The paper's density/luminosity scaling is conditional on a proposed Fermi association and assumed beaming/conversion efficiency; it is not a measured GW150914 disk. Rebuilding that setup generates a literature benchmark. Applying its disk to the differently spinning SXS realization creates a separate declared experiment, not a reproduction of Khan's result.

## The approximate binary metric

[Combi and Ressler, arXiv:2403.13308v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13308v3), 18 January 2026, constructs boosted superposed Kerr-Schild metrics with 4PN trajectories and a smooth transition to an NR-fitted Kerr remnant. This yields a metric evaluable at arbitrary requested coordinates without storing a complete spacetime grid. It is an approximation, not a numerical Einstein-equation solution. Section II.4 reserves accurate merger/GW prediction for numerical relativity. The reported average apparent-horizon mass/spin discrepancies of approximately 10% or less are not universal image, waveform or fluid error bounds. Its comparisons include uniform gas through merger and a separate magnetized circumbinary inspiral. This supports a carefully validated fluid/optical backend; it does not establish every binary-disk merger scenario.

The first actionable backend is an independent implementation of the paper's metric equations, starting with exact single-hole limits. Provide `g_cov(t,x)`, its inverse and all four coordinate derivatives, plus a checked conversion to lapse, shift, spatial metric and extrinsic curvature. Do not replace this contract with an inverse-square force or a product of two isolated redshift factors.

Use one trajectory/frame prescription for metric, bodies and fluid. For a new PN/SKS run, display its own centers and define its own capture/horizon approximation. Do not transplant puncture-gauge SXS tracks into Kerr-Schild coordinates without a justified transformation and calibration. The retained NR waveform can remain a clearly identified comparison when it is not the waveform of the generated metric. Custom changes to mass ratio, spins, separation or gas inputs need a new physical cache, unless a documented scale symmetry actually applies.

## Build sequence that generates the remaining fields

1. **Retain the current PDE experiment and viewer.** Add its settings, units, seeds, time interval, source forcing and mass-budget diagnostics to each generated cache. It is the low-cost numerical testbed and a usable educational mode.
2. **Implement and verify the metric in isolation.** Derive the SKS evaluator from the public mathematical specification, compare exact Kerr/Minkowski limits and independent derivatives, and add a declared trajectory and merger-fit provider. Mathematica is not required to evaluate independently implemented formulas. A simpler PN trajectory can be an intermediate test only if its lower order is named; it must not be labeled the paper's 4PN implementation.
3. **Build a small offline GRHD driver.** Use a pinned GRHayL non-tabulated/gamma-law configuration, an explicit finite-volume mesh and time integrator, and the verified metric interface. Generate uniform-flow, relativistic shock and single-hole accretion initial conditions ourselves. Start without magnetic fields. Primitive recovery, floor corrections and source terms are part of the algorithm and diagnostics.
4. **Advance GRHD on the moving metric.** Evaluate geometry at each integrator stage, include temporal metric effects and geometrical source terms, and use relativistic characteristic speeds for the timestep. Re-evaluating a stationary-metric callback once per image is insufficient. Choose boundaries/excision or interior treatment that makes the exterior calculation well posed. The approximate metric determines the regime of the claim even if the fluid equations are solved accurately.
5. **Add GRMHD after the hydrodynamics gates pass.** Generate a divergence-compatible magnetic field from a chosen vector potential, evolve induction with a divergence-control method, and verify the magnetic benchmarks. Select a literature disk family and relax it before measuring merger behavior. Resolving bulk spiral flow is not evidence of resolving MRI turbulence or kinetic reconnection.
6. **Export generated fields for Unity.** Preserve a master scientific cache with grid/metric, units, gauge, time coverage, equation/closure choices, source revision and diagnostics. Produce separately validated runtime bricks containing the fields needed for the chosen emission model and velocity samples. Stream bounded neighboring time blocks; do not allocate the complete history on a laptop. Material tracers integrate the flow velocity; they do not replace the fluid solver.
7. **Add M6 observer transfer separately.** Trace null rays through the same metric and sample emission at the retarded times encountered. Define emitter and camera four-velocities, local frames and proper clocks. [Bohn et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7775v3), Section II, supplies the relevant analytic/numerical-metric ray-tracing formulation. Preserve the previous coordinate inspection mode as a separate display.

An ET alternative can use our own metric adapter to populate [ADMBase](https://einsteintoolkit.org/thornguide/EinsteinBase/ADMBase/documentation.html) and a pinned current [IllinoisGRMHD](https://einsteintoolkit.org/thornguide/GRHayLET/IllinoisGRMHD/documentation.html). Correct lapse, shift, spatial metric, extrinsic curvature, stage scheduling and regridding updates are essential. This route can reuse established grid/AMR machinery; it still needs a complete generated initial-data setup and its own validation. For a full Einstein-equation evolution, the [public GW150914 ET example](https://einsteintoolkit.org/gallery/bbh/index.html) demonstrates the initial-data and merger workflow, rather than supplying a gas environment.

## Acceptance gates

These are initial proposed gates for the new relativistic route. Record actual results against a fixed input manifest. Tolerances apply to well-conditioned exterior samples and the specified benchmark, not to singularities or an unbounded domain. Changes to thresholds require an explained accuracy/scope decision, not a silent adjustment after a failed test.

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| G0: reduced PDE | Uniform-state and manufactured/advection cases; positive finite density; CFL enforcement; mass residual after outflow, sinks and floor additions below `1e-8 * max(initial mass, total added mass)` in double-precision controlled tests. Momentum budgets include external forcing. Isothermal Euler does not acquire an energy-conservation claim. |
| G1: metric | Minkowski and isolated Kerr limits; symmetric metric; Lorentzian signature and valid slicing outside the declared mask; normalized `g * inverse(g) - I` residual at most `1e-10` on regular double-precision fixtures. Compare derivatives at three finite-difference spacings and require convergence before choosing their tolerance. Record the nonzero Einstein/constraint residual of SKS as model error; grid refinement must not be claimed to eliminate it. |
| G2: GRHD | Three-resolution smooth-flow error with observed order at least 0.8 for a first-order scheme; relativistic shocks with positive states and decreasing L1 errors; primitive-to-conserved round trips with conditioned relative error at most `1e-9`; no unaccounted recovery corrections. Single-hole Bondi accretion rate within 2% on the finest grid and improving under refinement. |
| G3: binary fluid | Spatial and temporal refinement studies; complete conserved mass/flux ledger; all atmosphere, sink and recovery additions recorded; no NaN/Inf or unhandled unphysical state. Compare a common-clock slice before and after seek/regeneration. Compare integral diagnostics in the same geometry/frame; unrelated coordinate-radius profiles are not a validation reference. |
| G4: MHD | Flat-space magnetic benchmarks and magnetized accretion; consistent divergence diagnostics across grid/refinement boundaries; convergence under refinement. Declare the norm, normalization and method-specific threshold before the run. An MRI claim additionally requires reported wavelength/grid quality and adequate vertical/azimuthal coverage, not merely visible turbulence. |
| G5: export | Reopen every generated file; verify hashes, shapes, field conventions and coverage. Against master data target less than 1% errors in selected column-density/optical-depth integrals and less than `0.01c` absolute coordinate-velocity interpolation error over the admitted exterior domain. Verify timelike recovered states. These initial error budgets do not establish image error by themselves. |
| G6: optics and clocks | Flat, Schwarzschild and Kerr references; a distant-observer Schwarzschild shadow scale within 0.5% of `3 sqrt(3) M`; conditioned null residual at most `1e-6`; stationary frequency/clock fixtures within 0.1%; refinement and independent binary-ray comparison. Every ray must retain valid metric/emission history for its full light path. Successful straight-ray emission does not pass this gate. |
| G7: device admission | Measure generation and playback separately. Bound disk/cache/CPU/GPU allocations before starting; measure peaks and completed frame distributions on an actual target laptop and desktop. Test cancellation, interrupted-cache rejection, seeking, unload/reload and fallback. GPU counts or a desktop running a Laptop preset cannot substitute for a laptop run. |

Passing individual gates supports the corresponding model, not automatic completion of all later milestones. Genuine two-dimensional Newtonian hydrodynamics remains a reduced physical model; full M5 requires compatible, validated physical fluid history for the declared scene, and M6 requires the metric/observer transfer and clock checks.

## Compute and memory expectations

Keep scientific generation outside Unity's rendering loop. For a uniform 3D explicit scheme, doubling linear resolution increases cell count by eight and typically approximately doubles the number of timesteps for the same physical duration: approximately sixteen times the work before other effects. AMR, coordinate choice, stage count and recovery failures change the actual cost.

As simple storage arithmetic, eight float32 fields on a 128-cubed grid occupy 64 MiB per snapshot; 200 uncompressed snapshots occupy 12.5 GiB. Ten metric components, ten inverse components and forty derivatives in float64 already require 960 MiB at that resolution if all are stored simultaneously. Fluid state, buffers, ghost zones, AMR metadata and diagnostics add more. Evaluating analytic metric components in blocks and retaining only needed quantities can reduce this footprint; profile rather than assuming every quantity must remain resident.

The ET gallery's reference binary run reports 98 GB and approximately 8700 core-hours for its stated setup. Combi-Ressler's Table I measures a cluster calculation on 1920 CPUs. These establish the scale of those experiments, not a price or runtime for our reduced grid. A small GRHD proof can be much smaller; no production resolution, wall time or cloud charge should be promised before a pilot measures cells advanced per second, memory and output volume.

The next concrete scientific deliverable should therefore be a **tested standalone metric evaluator and single-hole GRHD benchmark**, followed by one short generated binary-flow pilot. This produces our own missing fields using public resources while preserving a reviewable distinction between working reduced fluid motion, relativistic modeling and observer imagery.
